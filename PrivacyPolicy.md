# Calendar Dashboard Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** 23 September 2026

mackiea ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Google Workspace Marketplace application, Calendar Dashboard (the "Application").

## 1. Information We Collect
Our Application interacts with your Google Workspace data using Google OAuth API scopes. We only request permissions that are strictly necessary for the Application's core functionality. 

Depending on the features you use, the Application may access:
* **User Profile Information:** Your name and email address to authenticate your identity.
* **Application-Specific Data:** Google Calendar read permissions to generate a calendar view.

## 2. How We Use Your Data
The application processes your data solely to provide the features of the Application. Specifically, we use your data to:
* Generate a visual display of your calendar events.
